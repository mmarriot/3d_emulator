Trackster metrics: emulated CLUEstering against truth targets

Base particles (calorimeter-boundary particles of the signal and every in-time pileup interaction) are first split into spatial fragments (deposits more than 0.05 in x/|z|, y/|z| apart, ~16 cm at the front face: a particle that radiated before the calorimeter, or a hadron's far satellites, owns separate impacts). Each fragment is a target, except when it is completely overlapped: with M(a,b) = Σl min(s(l,a), s(l,b)) / min(Ea, Eb) the part of the smaller one lying underneath the other in their layer clusters, a fragment with M ≥ τ to a harder one is part of that one's target (the one covering it most). Every decision is between two fragments, nothing is recomputed on groups, and partial overlaps never add up: a target is one shower plus what is hidden inside it. Targets with reachable Et ≥ the selection energy are scored.
