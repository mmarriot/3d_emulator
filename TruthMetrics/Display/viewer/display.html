Truth graph levels: single π⁺, 0 and 200 PU

Pick a truth level. Each particle in that set becomes one truth object with its own colour, and every calorimeter sim hit is credited to the nearest member above the particle that made it. Show sim hits, layer clusters or tracksters: a reco object takes the colour of the truth object that gave it most sim energy. Click nodes in the particle graph to select them (again to deselect); the display then keeps only what the selection touches, and the Selection panel adds up its energy and which reco objects capture it.
